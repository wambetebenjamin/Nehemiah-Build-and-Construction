A successful build starts with a shared understanding of the brief. Before drawings or materials are discussed, take time to agree how the property should work, who will use it, and what matters most.

## Start with the site

A site visit helps the project team discuss access, levels, utilities, neighbouring conditions, and questions that may need specialist advice. Record assumptions early; they are easier to resolve before work begins.

## Keep decisions visible

Agree who can approve changes, how costs are reviewed, and how progress will be reported. If you are building from abroad, a regular written update and a named local contact can make decisions less stressful.

## Plan for the full journey

Design coordination, approvals, procurement, construction, inspections, and handover are connected stages. Timings and requirements differ by project and county, so confirm current requirements with the relevant professionals and authorities.
