A material should suit the purpose of the space, the maintenance plan, and the conditions it will face. The lowest purchase price is not always the lowest lifetime cost.

## Ask about performance and care

Discuss exposure to rain, sun, dust, moisture, and daily wear with your architect or engineer. Ask suppliers for installation guidance, maintenance needs, and documentation for the exact product being specified.

## Check supply before committing

Confirm lead times, delivery access, replacement availability, and how price changes will be handled. A beautiful specification is only practical if the right quantities can arrive when the programme needs them.

## Make samples part of the decision

Review finish samples in the space where they will be used. Lighting, colour, texture, and cleaning requirements can look very different in a showroom than they do on site.

Every project is different. Ask the relevant design professional to confirm material suitability before procurement.
